FAQ

Questions We Get a Lot.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?
+
No contract, ever. All packages are month-to-month. If it's not working for you, you can cancel anytime. We want to earn your business every single month.
How do you communicate? Do you call?
+
Email only. We keep everything clear and documented in writing. You'll hear from us regularly with updates, reports, and questions — no surprise phone calls, no meetings unless you want one.
Do you offer content in Spanish?
+
Yes. We write in both English and Spanish. Tell us your preference and we match it. If you want bilingual content — both languages — we do that too.
What if I want to upgrade or downgrade my package?
+
Easy. Just email us and we'll adjust at the start of your next billing cycle. No penalties, no complications. Your package should match where your business is right now.
Do you work with businesses outside Texas?
+
Absolutely. We serve clients nationwide. Marketing is digital — it doesn't matter where you're located. If you have customers and need to reach more of them online, we can help.
What is the Ads Management package? Do I pay for the ads too?
+
The $597/mo covers everything we do: strategy, creative, setup, optimization, and reporting. The ad spend budget — what you actually pay Facebook or Google to run the ads — is separate and goes directly to the platform. We recommend a minimum of $300-500/mo in ad spend to see real results.
How long before I see results?
+
Organic marketing (social, GBP, SEO) typically takes 60-90 days to build momentum. Paid ads can generate leads much faster — sometimes within the first week. Either way, you'll get a full report every month so you can see exactly what's happening.
